RESEARCH

Research that shaped how I think about data.

My undergraduate research explored how machine learning can be used to understand learning and engagement through EEG signals.

IEEE PUBLICATION FIRST AUTHOR

Learning Behavior Analysis for Personalized E-Learning using EEG Signals

My undergraduate research focused on analyzing EEG signals to study classroom engagement and fatigue. I explored recurrent neural networks, including LSTM-based approaches, to identify meaningful patterns in the recorded signals.

The study achieved 96% classification accuracy across data collected from 30 participants, providing an early experience in working with real-world physiological data and applying machine learning to a research problem.

METHODS

EEG · RNN · LSTM · Signal Processing
