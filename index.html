LESSONS

Things I learned the hard way.

Let code do the math and the LLM do the talking.

An LLM can make an answer sound right even when the numbers behind it are thin. In our chatbot, the statistics come from code, and the model explains them.

Check the data before blaming the model.

The biggest problem in the chatbot was the data itself: some of it was incomplete, and some of it was slightly biased.

A trained model is only the beginning.

On the stress-test platform, parts that worked in development broke in deployment. Testing, CI/CD and monitoring are how we found and fixed them.

Fluent captions can still be wrong.

In VisionScript, full fine-tuning gave richer descriptions, and also more incorrect ones. Reading the actual outputs side by side is how we caught it.
